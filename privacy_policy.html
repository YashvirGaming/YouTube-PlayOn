Privacy Policy for NeverPause

Last updated: 2026

1. Data Collection

The NeverPause extension ("the Extension") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or analytics. Everything runs entirely locally on your device.

2. Permissions Used

The Extension requests permissions to run scripts on *://*.youtube.com/*. This permission is strictly utilized to detect and click the "Continue watching" popup on the page. No other interaction is made with the webpage.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or external APIs. There is no telemetry built into this extension.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support tab or the GitHub repository.
