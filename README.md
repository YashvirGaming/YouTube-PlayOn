The ultimate, lightweight extension that automatically clicks the **"Continue watching?"** popup for you.

Whether you're listening to a 10-hour lofi study mix, watching a massive documentary, or just leaving a stream on in the background, you shouldn't have to babysit your browser.

## ✨ Why Choose YouTube PlayOn?

- ⚡ **Zero Configuration** – Install it and forget it.
- 🪶 **Extremely Lightweight** – Uses efficient DOM observers and remains idle until the popup appears.
- 🔒 **Privacy Focused** – No tracking. No external servers. Everything runs locally.
- 🔄 **Future-Proof** – Built using Manifest V3 with multiple fallback detection methods.

Say goodbye to silent rooms when your music randomly stops.
