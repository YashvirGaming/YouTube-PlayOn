<h1>🚀 YouTube PlayOn</h1>

<h3><i>Never let YouTube stop your videos again.</i></h3>

<p><b>YouTube PlayOn</b> is a <b>fast</b>, <b>lightweight</b>, and <b>privacy-focused</b> Chrome extension that automatically clicks the <b>"Continue watching?"</b> popup—so your videos, music, livestreams, and playlists keep playing without interruption.</p>

## 🆕 **What's New in v1.1**

- 🛠️ **Fixed DOM Detection:** Updated selector logic to immediately catch and auto-dismiss all variations of YouTube's idle prompts.
- 📊 **Counter Reliability:** Fixed an issue where the lifetime "Prompts Dismissed" counter was not incrementing.

## ✨ **Why Choose YouTube PlayOn?**

Whether you're listening to a **10-hour lo-fi playlist**, watching a **long documentary**, following a **livestream**, or simply letting videos play in the background, **YouTube PlayOn** ensures your viewing experience stays uninterrupted.

### ⚡ **Zero Configuration**

No complicated setup.  
Simply **install the extension** and let it work automatically in the background.

---

### 🪶 **Extremely Lightweight**

Built with modern browser APIs and efficient **DOM MutationObservers**, the extension stays idle until the exact moment the popup appears—keeping CPU and memory usage to a minimum.

---

### 🔒 **Privacy First**

Your privacy matters.

- ✅ No analytics
- ✅ No data collection
- ✅ No external servers
- ✅ Everything runs **100% locally** inside your browser

---

### 🚀 **Built for the Future**

Designed using **Chrome Manifest V3** with multiple detection methods to remain reliable even as YouTube updates its interface.

---
