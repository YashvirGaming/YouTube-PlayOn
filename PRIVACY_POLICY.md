# Privacy Policy for YouTube PlayOn

**Last updated:** 2026

---

## 1. Data Collection

The **YouTube PlayOn** extension ("the Extension") does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or analytics.

Everything runs entirely locally on your device.

---

## 2. Permissions Used

The Extension requests permission to run scripts on:

```
*://*.youtube.com/*
```

This permission is used solely to detect and click the **"Continue watching"** popup on YouTube.

No other interaction is performed with the webpage.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Extension does **not** use:

- Third-party tracking
- Analytics
- External APIs
- Telemetry

---

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page.
